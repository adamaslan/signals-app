## Read this as

- A verdict is about this universe, this 5-year window and this horizon only. 5 years of a mostly rising market makes any long signal's baseline high and any short signal's low.
- Hit rate ignores magnitude. A signal can hit more often and still lose money.
- The full-history row lets indicators warm up on all prior bars. The 63-bar row, where computable, is the production scan's view.
- Survivorship: the universe is today's ticker list, so delisted names are absent.
- Definition used: ExpandedMACrossDetector label GOLDEN CROSS (SMA_50 over SMA_200), run bar by bar on causal views.
- Power: the 95% interval is wider than the goal-to-kill gap, so "KILL" means the point estimate fell under the pre-registered line, not that an edge of the goal size is excluded. Intervals are wide because tickers and months are resampled independently, which is the point of the two-way bootstrap.
- Evidence: run locally against yfinance 5y daily bars cached on the laptop (940 of 954 universe tickers; 14 had no data or too little history, including BBBY, MIDZ and other delisted or new symbols). Not a CI run. The 63-bar window is not computable for this series, so only the full-history window was evaluated.
