What runs where, and what this PR changes

signals-app splits its work three ways. The scan and the published signals live in the cloud. Everything personal stays in your browser. A full local pipeline runs on a dev machine for free dry runs. This page maps each feature to one of those places and summarises what branch feat/landing-signal-guide adds.

What the new section explains

18 detectors in five families

The families come from the scorer (scoring/families.py). Detectors in a family measure the same underlying fact, so agreement across families counts for more than agreement within one.

4TrendIs price moving in a sustained direction?MA crosses & stack · MA cross grid · ADX · Ichimoku
7MomentumIs the move speeding up or fading?RSI · multi-RSI · MACD · MACD variants · stochastic ×2 · large move

Research · docs/2000ticker.md

A second universe of 2,000 tickers

Start from the free Nasdaq Trader symbol lists. Keep US-listed common stock, and drop ETFs, warrants, units, preferreds and anything already in the 954 seed. 4,653 candidates remain. Rank them by 20-day median dollar volume and keep the top 2,000.

The whole pool downloaded from yfinance in 271 seconds, so fetching isn't the constraint. The cost is LLM calls: about 660–840 more per daily run at today's 33–42% publish rate.

The build is split into four PRs: fix the landing truncation, add a seed builder and file, add a separate 8-shard CI scan job, and add a second auto-seeded browser universe.

Open issues

Critical

The AI explanation step is failing on almost every signal

The 2026-09-23 scan shows every published signal at exactly 55% confidence. The rule-based fallback hard-codes that value (synthesis/mtf_llm.py). The app overview already lists OPENROUTER_API_KEY as unset (P0). Until this is fixed, every deep dive reads "Rule-Based" and the landing page shows a degraded banner.

Before scaling

The landing page reads at most 1,000 rows, in no set order

fetchLandingSignals uses limit(1000) with no ordering on the view. Fine at 954 tickers; with a second universe the landing page would compute from an arbitrary subset without any error.

Docs drift

COMPLETE_FEATURES_GUIDE lists detectors that don't exist

It names "RSI Divergence", "Pivot Point Breakout" and "Volatility Breakout", none of which appear in get_default_detectors(). The new glossary follows the code; the guide needs a pass.
